# The course
{: .no_toc }

About 25 short lessons, each around 20 minutes. Everyone takes Modules 0–2 together, then picks a path: **Clinic** or **Lab**. Everyone finishes with the capstone. You can stop after any lesson and pick up exactly where you left off.

## How it works

You work inside **Antigravity IDE**, a free app with your files, an editor, and an AI agent in one window. An **AI tutor** lives inside the course folder. Type `/start-course` and it guides you one step at a time, checks your work, and saves your place. You do the typing and clicking yourself, because doing it is how you learn it.

Everything happens at a fictional place: the **University of Tomorrow's Department of Experimental Theoretical Psychology**. The emails, meetings and messy shared drives are made up, so you can practice without risk.

**The one idea that runs through it all:** an AI agent is **instructions + skills + memory**, written in plain files you can read and change.

## Module 0: Getting started

| Lesson | What you do |
|---|---|
| 0.1 Welcome to the department | Meet your tutor, find your way around, and make your first AI edit. |
| 0.2 What never goes into an AI tool | Sort real-world scenarios: what's safe to paste, and what never is. |

## Module 1: Foundations

| Lesson | What you do |
|---|---|
| 1.1 Finding your way around the editor | Make files and folders, and review an AI's changes before you keep them. |
| 1.2 Read, think, act | Decide how much the AI should do on its own: look, plan, then act on a copy. |
| 1.3 Giving the AI context | Point the AI at the right file, and ask with context, task, format and constraints. |
| 1.4 First real tasks | Turn a rambling meeting into action items, and draft (then fix) a reply to the department chair. |

## Module 2: Making it reusable

| Lesson | What you do |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Templates and rules | Save your format, and write a rule the AI follows every time. |
| 2.2 Open the hood | Open the files that run your tutor, change its voice, and see why it remembers you. |
| 2.3 Build your first skill | Build `/meeting-to-actions`: your own procedure, run on command. |
| 2.4 When the AI asks to run a command | Read a command before you approve it. |

## Then choose a path

### Clinic path (the Daybreak Clinic)

No client information, ever. These are projects you could use in your own practice tomorrow.

| Lesson | What you do |
|---|---|
| 3A.1 Meet the clinic director | Turn a rambling voice memo into a project brief. Build a `/tidy-up` skill. |
| 3A.2a–b Build a chief of staff | An agent that plans the director's week, preps meetings, and leaves itself handoff notes. |
| 3A.3a–b Vibe code a clinic website | Build a one-page site by describing it, then check it for accuracy, professional ethics, accessibility, and who it leaves out. |

### Lab path (the Foresight Lab)

Real, openly licensed research data and papers.

| Lesson | What you do |
|---|---|
| 3B.1 Meet the lab | Turn vague requests into projects with a clear question each. Build a `/tidy-up` skill. |
| 3B.2a–b Build a lab agent | An agent that builds a literature matrix and cites only what it was given. Then the "five more references" trap. |
| 3B.3a–b Vibe code an analysis | Set the question first, check who's in the sample, run the analysis in Python, then compare it with the published, peer-reviewed version. |

## Module 4: Capstone

| Lesson | What you do |
|---|---|
| 4.1 Moving day | Move your agent out of the course into a folder of its own. |
| 4.2 Go live | A go-live checklist (data, policy, your red lines), then make the agent yours. |
| 4.3 Pick your project | Choose from the idea gallery, or invent your own, and scope it with your agent. |
| 4.4 Build it | Build it in 20-minute chunks. Your agent remembers where you left off. |
| 4.5 Show it | Show someone, check it before anything goes public, and look back. |
