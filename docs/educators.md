# For educators
{: .no_toc }

The course is free and openly licensed, so you can use it as it is or adapt it for your program. This is a short guide. If a fuller one (rubrics, facilitation tips) would help your program, [tell us in the feedback form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScOa7OC-TLVinv5Ljma0d_PRYZFlxe_HaswHk-rMUKGWFmj3w/viewform).

## Where it fits

- **Practicum or clinical seminar:** the Clinic path.
- **Research methods or a stats lab:** the Lab path.
- **Professional development** for clinicians or faculty.
- **First-year orientation:** Modules 0–2 only.

## Session plans

- **Four sessions:** Modules 0–1 · Module 2 · a path · the capstone and a share-out.
- **One workshop:** Modules 0–2 in a three-hour block.

Lessons are about 20 minutes each and self-paced, so learners can also work between sessions.

## Before you assign it

- **Cost:** the course is free; it runs on Antigravity's free plan, which has weekly usage limits.
- **Policy:** check your institution's AI and data policies.
- **The rule:** make sure students understand it uses no real patient or participant data, ever.
- **Outside the US:** Lesson 0.2 and the capstone's go-live checklist use US examples (HIPAA, IRBs). Adapt them to your local law and ethics framework. That's the real localization work, more than translation.

## Assessment ideas

- The capstone project as an assignment.
- The capstone retrospective: what the learner would and wouldn't trust an agent with.
- Peer review of each other's skills.

## Adapting it

The content is **CC BY-SA 4.0** and the code is **MIT**. You can fork the [repository](https://github.com/dccooper/agentic-psychologist), swap in your own clinic or lab scenario and dataset, or add lessons. Keep the attribution, and share your changes under the same license.
