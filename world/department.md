# The University of Tomorrow
## Department of Experimental Theoretical Psychology

*Everything in this folder is fictional. Any resemblance to real people, institutions, or clients is coincidental.*

### The department

The Department of Experimental Theoretical Psychology at the University of Tomorrow is a small, slightly chaotic, very well-meaning graduate department. Its unofficial motto, painted over the door of the faculty lounge, is **"Tomorrow's Treatments, Today's Stress."**

Things you'll come to know: **Procrastination Hall** (opening next semester, they say), the Office of Research Compliance (**Ward, Locke & Key**), and a faculty lounge coffee machine that's been "temporarily out of order" since before the founding of the department.

The department runs two things that matter to you:

- **The Daybreak Clinic:** the department's community training clinic, where trainees see clients under supervision. → [clinic.md](clinic.md)
- **The Foresight Lab:** a research lab studying how people imagine and plan for the future. → [lab.md](lab.md)

Like most departments, it's under-staffed and over-scheduled, and it has lots of good ideas that never quite get organized. That's where you come in.

### You

You're a **new first-year trainee** starting this week. You'll spend time in both the clinic and the lab before choosing where to focus. Your peer mentor, Jordan, has promised to "show you the ropes," which mostly means forwarding you a lot of messy files.

### Tone

Vaudeville-era comedy: Groucho Marx, the Three Stooges, *Dewey, Cheatham & Howe*. Puns, absurd institutional names, deadpan memos, and running gags.

- **The humor lives in the world** (emails, transcripts, signage, characters). **Tutor instructions stay clear and plain.** A nervous learner should never have to decode a joke to find out what to do next.
- **Punch at institutions, never at clients, patients, or mental illness.** The joke is bureaucracy, deadlines, and the coffee machine, never the people being served.
- A light touch: about one gag per artifact. It seasons the material, it isn't the meal.

### How the course uses this world

- **Modules 0–2** happen in the department: orientation, meetings, emails, getting organized.
- **Module 3A (Clinic path)** happens at the Daybreak Clinic. You'll help run the *business* of the clinic. **The clinic path never uses client information**, even fictional client records, except where a lesson clearly marks synthetic examples.
- **Module 3B (Lab path)** happens in the Foresight Lab, using openly licensed public data.
- **Module 4 (Capstone)** leaves the University of Tomorrow behind. You'll build something for your own real (non-patient) work.
