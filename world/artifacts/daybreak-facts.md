# Daybreak Clinic: Current Facts

- **Name:** The Daybreak Clinic, a community training clinic of the Department of Experimental Theoretical Psychology, University of Tomorrow.
- **Who we are:** a sliding-scale clinic. Services are provided by **doctoral trainees under the supervision of licensed psychologists.** (This must be stated clearly.)
- **Services:**
  - Individual therapy for adults and adolescents (13+)
  - Groups (current groups change each semester; ask the front desk. **There is no evening group.**)
  - **Psychological assessment:** learning, attention (including ADHD), and cognitive assessments for children, adolescents, and adults
- **Fees:** sliding scale based on household income. No one is turned away for inability to pay the lowest fee.
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 9:00 am–5:00 pm. **Closed weekends.**
- **Phone:** (555) 010-0142 *(new number; the basement line is retired)*
- **Address:** Room 104, Department of Experimental Theoretical Psychology, University of Tomorrow. Parking: visitor lot B (free with a validated ticket from the front desk).
- **Languages:** services in English. Spanish-language services are available on some days; call to ask.
- **For providers making a referral:** call the front desk, or send a referral form (on request). We aim to contact referred clients within one week.
- **In a crisis:** we are not a crisis service. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number.
