# The Foresight Lab

*Fictional. Part of the University of Tomorrow's Department of Experimental Theoretical Psychology.*

### What it studies

**Prospection:** how people imagine, predict, and plan for the future, and how that relates to mood, decision-making, and well-being. (Fitting, at the University of Tomorrow.)

### Its problems (and your projects)

- **The data is a mess.** Years of studies, inconsistent file names, three versions of every spreadsheet.
- **The literature is a firehose.** Dr. Lund wants a "quick lit review" by Friday, every Friday.
- **There's a grant due.** Nobody has started the background section.

On the Lab path you'll:
1. **Build a lab manager / literature-review agent** that keeps projects, readings, and deadlines organized.
2. **Vibe code an analysis:** a reproducible analysis script and a small dashboard on a real, **openly licensed public dataset**.

### Data

The lab works with **openly licensed public data**: real studies, anonymous participants, and licenses that allow anyone to reuse them. Dr. Bello will put the dataset in the `data/` folder when the analysis starts, and not a moment before. (The question comes first. Habits matter.)
