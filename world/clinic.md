# The Daybreak Clinic

*Fictional. Part of the University of Tomorrow's Department of Experimental Theoretical Psychology.*

### What it is

A small sliding-scale community clinic in the department's building, staffed by supervised trainees. It offers individual therapy, some group work, and psychological assessment.

### Its problems (and your projects)

The clinical work is good. The **operations** are held together with sticky notes:

- **Nobody can find anything.** Policies, forms, and schedules are scattered across emails and a shared drive no one understands.
- **Referrals have dried up.** The clinic website was last updated by a trainee who graduated years ago. Local providers don't know what the clinic offers or who it's for.
- **The director is drowning.** Dr. Reyes runs supervision, the budget, trainee schedules, community partnerships, and their own life, all from one overflowing inbox.

On the Clinic path you'll:
1. **Build a chief of staff agent** for Dr. Reyes: weekly planning, project tracking, meeting prep, and personal-life planning too.
2. **Vibe code a clinic marketing project:** a simple website or a referral one-pager generator for local providers.

### The rule

None of these projects need client information, and none of them use it. That's deliberate: these are the AI projects you can use in your own practice tomorrow without any privacy risk.
