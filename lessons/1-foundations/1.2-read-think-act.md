# Lesson 1.2: Read, Think, Act: How Much Should the AI Do on Its Own?

- **Module:** 1 (shared)
- **Time:** ~20 min
- **Request budget:** ~8 agent requests (estimate; verify in free-tier testing)
- **Last tested:** not yet tested

## Goal

The learner can choose how much to let the AI do, based on risk: **read** (look, don't touch) → **think** (propose a plan) → **act** (make changes, on a copy, then review). And they know the difference between **Accept all** and automatic approval.

## Why it matters to a psychologist

The same habit as clinical judgment: you don't act before you've assessed and made a plan. An AI that can change files deserves the same order of operations, especially near anything that matters.

## Setup

- `world/artifacts/okafor-shared-drive-email.md` (Dr. Okafor asks for a proposal: "don't move anything yet")
- `world/artifacts/shared-drive/`: about 10 badly named dummy files in `FINAL`, `FINAL_actual`, and `USE THIS ONE`. **The original stays untouched.** All changes happen on a practice copy in `workspace/`.

## UI this lesson depends on

See `lessons/SCREEN_GUIDE.md`: opening files; reviewing changes (the **Review** card, **Files With Changes**, **Accept all**); the review setting chosen at setup (**Strict / Review-driven / Agent-driven**). ⚠️ *Where to change that setting after setup isn't verified.* In step 6, ask the learner to look for it and report what they find. Copying files may make the agent ask to **run a command**. If so, see the sticking points.

**Teaching note (Antigravity-specific):** Antigravity has no separate "ask mode" button like some editors. The learner controls how much the AI does in two ways: **what they ask for** ("look but don't change anything," "write a plan," "now do it") and **the review setting**. Teach the idea, not a button.

## Steps

1. **Read the request.** Have them open `world/artifacts/okafor-shared-drive-email.md`. Ask them what Dr. Okafor wants, and what she *doesn't* want yet.
   - ✅ **Checkpoint:** they notice "a proposal" and "**don't move anything yet**." (Expected answer: she wants a plan for tidying the drive, and no files moved.)

2. **READ: look, don't touch.** Have them ask the AI, in their own words, to look through `world/artifacts/shared-drive/` and tell them what's in it and what's duplicated or confusing, **without changing anything**. Suggest they say "don't change any files" explicitly. Answer as asked, and change nothing.
   - ✅ **Checkpoint:** they got a description, and no **Files With Changes** bar appeared. Point that out: *"Nothing to approve, because nothing changed. That's what 'read' looks like."*

3. **THINK: ask for a plan, not an action.** Have them ask the AI to **write a proposal** for reorganizing the drive into `workspace/drive-plan.md` (new folder names, which file goes where, which duplicates to keep). *Why:* a plan is cheap to change. Moved files are not.
   - Write the plan. It's one new file, so they review and accept it like in 1.1.
   - ✅ **Checkpoint:** `workspace/drive-plan.md` exists and they've read it.

4. **Edit the plan yourself.** Ask them to change at least one thing in `drive-plan.md` by hand (a folder name they'd prefer, a file they'd keep instead). *Why:* the plan is theirs. The AI drafted it. **Pause point here.**
   - ✅ **Checkpoint:** they made an edit and saved it.

5. **ACT: on a copy, then review.** Have them ask the AI to **copy** `world/artifacts/shared-drive/` to `workspace/shared-drive-practice/` and reorganize *the copy* following `workspace/drive-plan.md`.
   - Do exactly that. Never touch `world/artifacts/shared-drive/`.
   - Many files will change at once. Have them use the **Files With Changes** list to look through them: are the files where the plan said? Did anything disappear that shouldn't have? Then decide.
   - ✅ **Checkpoint:** `workspace/shared-drive-practice/` is organized, and **the original in `world/artifacts/shared-drive/` is unchanged**. Ask them to check the original themselves in the Explorer.

6. **How much should the AI do on its own?** A short conversation, about two minutes:
   - *"You've clicked Accept a lot by now. Reviewing everything gets tiring, and tired people click Accept without looking. That's how mistakes slip through."*
   - **The rule of thumb:** *look closely at anything that changes your work or does something new. Glance-and-accept routine bookkeeping (like `progress.md`).*
   - **The key distinction:** **Accept all** approves what's on screen *now*, after you've looked. **Automatic approval** (the *Agent-driven* setting from setup) approves *future* edits you haven't seen. The course keeps review on.
   - Ask them to find where Antigravity lets them change the review setting (try **Antigravity - Settings** in the bottom-right corner), and tell you what they see. **Don't have them change it.** Record what they found in `progress.md` Notes for the course authors.

7. **Name the moment.** Two sentences: *"Read, think, act. You matched how much the AI could do to how risky the step was, and you acted on a copy first. That's the same judgment you'd use with any new tool near real work."*

## Pause points

- After step 4 (plan written and edited): a natural break before acting.
- End of lesson.

## Done when

`workspace/drive-plan.md` exists with at least one learner edit; `workspace/shared-drive-practice/` is reorganized per the plan; the original is untouched; and they can say the difference between Accept all and automatic approval.

## Common sticking points

- **The AI changes files during the "read" step** → a perfect teaching moment. Reject the changes, and point out that saying "don't change anything" matters, and so does reviewing.
- **The AI asks to run a command** (e.g., to copy files) → have them read the command first. Copy commands are fine. Anything that deletes, or touches a folder outside `workspace/`, gets a **Reject** and a question. Lesson 2.4 covers the terminal properly.
- **Too many changes to review comfortably** → that's the point of step 6. Have them spot-check a few files against the plan, then decide.
- **They want to fix the real `shared-drive`** → no. Dr. Okafor said "don't move anything yet," and acting on a copy first is the lesson.

## Avoid

- Don't touch `world/artifacts/shared-drive/`, ever.
- Don't have the learner switch to Agent-driven or any automatic-approval setting.
- Don't call any control "Ask mode" or "Plan mode." Antigravity doesn't label them that way. Use "ask it to only look" and "ask it for a plan."
- Don't lecture on approval fatigue for more than two or three sentences.
