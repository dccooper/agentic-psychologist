# Go-Live Checklist

*Copy this file to `workspace/capstone/go-live.md` and fill it in **before** your agent touches any real information (Lesson 4.2). Answer in your own words. "I don't know" is an honest answer, and it means "find out first."*

## 1. What data will this touch?

List it. (Your calendar? To-dos? Reading notes? Colleagues' names? Drafts?)

-

## 2. Where does it go?

- Everything you type or attach goes to the AI model's provider (for Antigravity, Google). What does your Antigravity **data-use setting** say? (You chose it at setup.)
- Is your agent's folder inside a **cloud-synced** folder (OneDrive, iCloud, Dropbox, Google Drive)? If so, it goes there too. Is that allowed for this data?

-

## 3. Institutional AI policy

Does your school or employer have an AI policy? Does it allow this tool for this work? Paste the link.

-

## 4. Protected data: the stop sign

Will this ever touch **patient or client information (PHI)** or **identifiable research data**? If yes: **stop.** That needs a BAA, IRB approval, or an approved tool, decided with your institution. Not this setup, and not improvised.

- [ ] This agent will not touch protected data.

## 5. Review before accept

Review stays on. What will you look at closely (anything that changes your real files), and what can you glance at (bookkeeping)?

-

## 6. My red line

One or two sentences your agent must never cross. These go into your agent's `AGENTS.md` word for word.
*(Example: "No client names, initials, or details, ever. Clinical time is written as 'clinical block'. Never paste a calendar export.")*

-

---

## Before anything goes public (Lesson 4.5)

Fill this in for any project you might put on the web.

- [ ] No passwords, API keys, or tokens anywhere in the folder.
- [ ] No memory files, handoff notes, `AGENTS.md`, or `.agents/` in the public folder.
- [ ] No personal details I wouldn't put on a flyer.
- [ ] No patient or participant data, ever.
- [ ] Nothing loaded from a site I don't recognize.
