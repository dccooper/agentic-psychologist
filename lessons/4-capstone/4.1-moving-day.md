# Lesson 4.1: Moving Day: Your Agent Gets Its Own Folder

- **Module:** 4 (capstone, shared)
- **Time:** ~20 min
- **Request budget:** ~6 agent requests (estimate; verify in free-tier testing)
- **Last tested:** not yet tested as a script (QA pass 5 ran an improvised version from the outline: the copy step worked)

## Goal

The learner moves their path agent (chief of staff or lab agent) **out of the course** into a folder of its own, where its instructions become that folder's `AGENTS.md`, and has a first conversation with it **without the professor**.

## Why it matters to a psychologist

The University of Tomorrow was practice. An agent you'll actually use should live with your own work, not inside a course. Moving it by hand also proves the point of the whole course: an agent is just instructions + skills + memory, in files you can carry anywhere.

## Setup

- **This is the last fully guided lesson.** From 4.2 on, the professor coaches more and guides less (see `AGENTS.md`, "Hold their hand less as they go").
- **Which agent moves:** the chief of staff (`workspace/clinic/chief-of-staff/` + skills `weekly-plan`, `meeting-prep`, `wrap-up`) or the lab agent (`workspace/lab/lab-agent/` + skills `lit-matrix`, `weekly-lab-update`, `lab-wrap-up`). **If they did both paths, they pick one.** The other can move later, on their own, with the same packing list.
- **Where it goes:** a folder **outside** the course folder (e.g., `Documents/my-agent`; the name is theirs), and **not** in a cloud-synced folder (OneDrive, iCloud, Dropbox) unless their go-live checklist in 4.2 says that's allowed. *Why outside:* inside the course folder, the professor's `AGENTS.md` could mix in ⚠️, and a fresh course download could overwrite it.
- **Copy, don't move**, so the course keeps working.
- **The professor doesn't see the new folder.** Everything the professor checks after the move, the learner brings back (a file list, a pasted reply). **Never mark this lesson done on the learner's say-so alone.**
- ✅ *QA pass 5:* the professor copied files to a folder outside the course with a small script (one approval). ⚠️ Unverified: **File → New Window** / **Open Folder** labels in a second window; whether the new window's `AGENTS.md` is read as always-on; whether `/` finds skills there.

## UI this lesson depends on

See `lessons/SCREEN_GUIDE.md`: command approval (explained first); **File → New Window** and **Open Folder** ⚠️; the trust prompt; **+** for a new conversation; switching between two Antigravity windows ⚠️.

## Steps

1. **The packing list.** Ask: *"Your agent is moving out. What has to come with it? Sort it by part."* Let them answer first. Expected:
   - **Instructions:** `instructions.md`, which becomes **`AGENTS.md`** in the new folder.
   - **Skills:** their three skills, into the new folder's `.agents/skills/`.
   - **Memory:** the `memory/` folder, which gets **reset** (the Dr. Reyes or Dr. Lund contents stay behind).
   - Outputs (`plans/`, `prep/`, `lit-matrix.md`, `analysis/`) stay behind as souvenirs.
   - ✅ **Checkpoint:** they named all three parts and what happens to each. (A good moment to suggest `/tidy-up` first, if they built it.)

2. **Make the new home.** Have them choose the folder and its name. Then they ask you to copy the files there. **Explain the command (or script) first** in one or two lines: it copies, it doesn't move or delete, and it only writes to the new folder. They approve once. Fallback: copy by hand in File Explorer or Finder.
   - ✅ **Checkpoint:** the copy finished without errors, and the course's own copies are still in `workspace/` (check them).

3. **Rename and reset.** In the new folder: `instructions.md` → `AGENTS.md`. Memory files keep their headings but lose the old contents, and `memory/handoff.md` says *"First session in the new home. Nothing yet."* **No real content goes in yet.** That waits for the go-live checklist in 4.2. *Why:* safety checks come before real data, not after.
   - ✅ **Checkpoint:** have them list the new folder's files (or ask you to list them, if you can reach it). Expected: `AGENTS.md`, `memory/` (reset), `.agents/skills/<three skills>/SKILL.md`, and no `instructions.md`.

4. **Before they go.** Save `progress.md` at "4.1, step 5: back from the agent's window." Then tell them exactly how to come back: *"When you're done there, switch back to this window and type `/next-step`."* **Pause point.**

5. **Open the new folder in a new window.** **File → New Window**, then **Open Folder** and choose the new folder ⚠️. Accept the trust prompt. One line: *"I won't be in that window. It's just you and your agent. That's on purpose."*

6. **First conversation, alone.** In the agent's window, they ask their agent, in plain words, to read its files and say who it is and what it can do. Then the first **fix by telling**: the skills still point to old course paths (`workspace/...`) and start with "read `instructions.md` first." They tell the agent, in their own words, to fix the paths, drop the "read instructions first" step (its `AGENTS.md` is now read automatically), and add a small **Commands** table to `AGENTS.md` mapping each `/command` to its skill. They review the changes.
   - ✅ **Checkpoint (when they're back, step 7):** they paste the agent's "who I am" reply, or describe it. It names its role and its three skills. **If it doesn't know its role**, `AGENTS.md` may not be read in that window ⚠️: note it in `progress.md` for the course authors.

7. **Back in the course window: `/next-step`.** Short debrief: what worked, what felt odd. Then the **knowledge check**: *"Why did your skills no longer need a 'read instructions.md first' step?"* (Expected: in its own folder, `AGENTS.md` is read automatically every time, like the professor's instructions in 2.2.)

8. **Name the moment.** Two sentences: *"You moved all three parts of an agent by hand, and it still works, because an agent is just files. The University of Tomorrow was practice; this one lives in your house now."*

## Pause points

- After step 4 (before switching windows).
- End of lesson.

## Done when

The new folder holds `AGENTS.md` (with a Commands table), reset `memory/`, and three skills with corrected paths; the course's copies are untouched; and the learner has **shown** (pasted or described) the agent's first reply in its own window.

## Common sticking points

- **They can't get back to the course window** → the taskbar (Windows) or Window menu / Cmd+` (Mac) ⚠️. The course window's title shows the course folder's name.
- **Open Folder replaced the course window** → reopen the course folder from **File → Open Recent**. Nothing is lost: progress lives in `progress.md`.
- **`/` shows no skills in the new window** → check the folder is `.agents/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` with frontmatter at the top; start a new conversation there.
- **They want to put it in OneDrive or Documents synced to the cloud** → hold that decision until the go-live checklist in 4.2.

## Avoid

- Don't put any real information in the agent yet. That's after the checklist in 4.2.
- Don't move or delete anything in the course's `workspace/`. Copy only.
- Don't claim the move worked without the learner's evidence from the new window.
